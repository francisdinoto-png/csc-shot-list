Morning Chat with John
Annie and John, behind the counter. Annie asks, John answers.

Camera setup
→

Question prompts
Q1
"John, what's the plan for today — walk me through what you're getting into."
Q2
"What's the part of today's install you're most focused on or most cautious about?"
Q3
"Is there anything about this car or setup that makes today different from a normal install?"
Q4
"What does a good day look like — what do you want done by the time we shut it down?"
Let him finish every answer. If he goes somewhere interesting, follow it — that's the content.
